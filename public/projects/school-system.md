# School Management System

## Project Overview
ERP platform designed for managing student lifecycles, payroll, attendance, and academic operations through modular backend services.

## Requirements
- Modular architecture to support various institutional needs.
- Role-based access control for students, teachers, and admins.
- High-performance reporting for academic and financial data.
- Scalable database design for large student populations.

## My Contributions
- **Module Design**: Architected the academic and payroll modules from scratch.
- **Reporting Engine**: Built a custom reporting system for generating complex academic transcripts and financial statements.
- **RBAC Implementation**: Designed and implemented the fine-grained permission system.

## Solutions
- Adopted a **modular monolithic architecture** that allows for independent development of institutional features.
- Used **Eloquent Relationships** and optimized Eager Loading to ensure efficient data retrieval for large reports.
- Implemented **custom caching strategies** for academic year configurations and holiday calendars.
